IN THE HIGH COURT OF DEMOLAND: Rao v. State (2098)

1. The petitioner seeks an anticipatory release order under Section 10 of the Demo Procedure Act, 2099 in connection with an alleged offence of cheating.

2. The Court holds that the power under Section 10 is not restricted to a fixed period. An order of anticipatory release may continue until the end of the trial unless the Court directs otherwise.

3. The Court observes that arrest must not be made as a matter of routine. The officer must record reasons in writing for every arrest, and the magistrate must examine those reasons before authorising detention.

4. The Court further holds that the applicant need not first be arrested before approaching the Court, because the whole purpose of Section 10 is to protect personal liberty before arrest takes place.

5. In the present case the petitioner has cooperated with the investigation and has surrendered his passport. The application is therefore allowed subject to the conditions in Section 11.
