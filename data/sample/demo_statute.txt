THE DEMO PROCEDURE ACT, 2099

Section 10. Anticipatory release order. When any person has reason to believe that he may be arrested on an accusation of a non-bailable offence, he may apply to the Regional Court for an order that, in the event of such arrest, he shall be released on bail.

Section 11. Conditions of anticipatory release. The Regional Court may impose conditions, including that the person shall make himself available for interrogation by an officer when required, and shall not leave the country without the permission of the Court.

Section 12. Regular bail in bailable offences. When any person accused of a bailable offence is arrested, he shall be released on bail on furnishing a bond, and the officer in charge shall inform him of this right.

Section 13. Time limit for investigation. If the investigation of an offence punishable with imprisonment up to seven years is not completed within sixty days of arrest, the accused shall be released on bail if he is prepared to furnish bail.

Section 14. Cancellation of bail. The Regional Court may direct that any person released on bail be arrested and committed to custody if he misuses his liberty or tampers with witnesses.

Section 15. Notice of arrest. An officer shall issue a written notice of appearance to a person against whom a reasonable complaint has been made, instead of arresting him, where the offence is punishable with imprisonment of less than seven years.
